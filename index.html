Privacy Policy, Zip Puzzle

Effective date: October 26, 2026

In short

  • Zip Puzzle has no account, no sign in and no server of our own. We do not receive your game data.
  • Your progress, best times, daily streak and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads using Google's ad SDK (Google AdMob). Google collects certain data, such as your IP address and advertising ID, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is described below.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked before personalized ads are used, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy choices.
  • An optional one time purchase removes ads between puzzles. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Zip Puzzle (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by manextapps. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. For questions, see Contact.

2. About the app

Zip Puzzle is a logic puzzle game: you draw one line through every cell of a grid and link the numbers in order. It includes numbered levels, a daily puzzle with a day streak, quick play by board size and difficulty, optional hints, themes and settings. Puzzles are created and played on your device. An internet connection is used only for ads, for the ad consent form, for the optional purchase, and when you choose to open a link such as this policy.

3. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not operate a server for this app and do not collect, receive or store personal data about you. The app has no account or sign in, no analytics or crash reporting tools of our own, and does not use Firebase.

Ads in the app are provided by Google, which collects some data directly through its software development kit (SDK). That is explained in section 5. The optional purchase is processed by Google Play, see section 8.

If you email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

4. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your device so the game works. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: your current level, best times per board size and difficulty, and how many quick play puzzles you have started.
  • Daily puzzle results and your daily streak.
  • Counters used to pace ads (for example, how many puzzles you solved since the last full screen ad).
  • Settings: theme, sound and vibration, and whether ads were removed by a purchase.

Google's ad, consent and billing libraries also store some information on your device, such as your consent choices, so that they work correctly.

5. Advertising and Google's ad SDK

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request ad consent where required. The app shows:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one to get a hint. If no ad is available, you still get the hint. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.
  • Full screen (interstitial) ads, only after you solve a puzzle, at most once every three solved puzzles and never during a puzzle or in your first session. These stop after the remove ads purchase.

Personalized and non personalized ads

Personalized ads are based on information such as your interests and past activity. Non personalized ads are based on more limited context, such as the app itself and your general (not precise) location. Depending on your choices, your region and your device settings, Google may show you personalized, non personalized or limited ads. Learn more in How Google uses cookies and similar technologies in advertising.

6. Consent and your ad choices

Where it is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and, where enabled, certain U.S. states), the app shows Google's consent message when it starts. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.

When privacy options are available for your region, you can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings, Privacy choices. If your choices mean ads cannot be requested, the game still works and hints are still available.

You can also manage ad personalization for your Google Account at My Ad Center.

7. Your advertising ID

Android provides a resettable advertising ID. On most devices you can reset or delete it in Settings, Privacy, Ads (menu names vary by device and Android version). Deleting it stops apps from accessing it for advertising; resetting it replaces it with a new random ID. Some devices also offer ad privacy settings for the Android Privacy Sandbox in the same area.

8. In app purchase

You can buy a one time “Remove ads” upgrade. The purchase is made through Google Play Billing: Google handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the upgrade belongs to your Google account, so it can turn off full screen ads and restore the purchase on a new device. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

9. Android permissions

The app does not ask you for runtime permissions such as location, camera, contacts or storage. The installed app includes the following permissions, which are added by Google's libraries:

  • Internet access and network state: to load ads, the consent form and the purchase screen.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad SDK read the advertising ID.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): let the ad SDK use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features where the device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app offer the optional purchase.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: required by the Android WorkManager library that the ad SDK uses for background tasks. The app itself does not run a foreground service.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the app are served by Google, which collects the data described in section 5. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions or concerns, please contact us.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Our servers: we have none, so we keep no copy of your game data.
  • On your device: game data stays until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Google: data collected by Google's ad SDK and by Google Play is retained according to Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google retains data.

12. Device backup

The app allows Android's backup feature to include your game progress and settings, so they can be restored if you set up a new device. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may store this data in your Google Account backup or move it during a device to device transfer, under Google's terms and your device settings. Data stored by the ad and consent libraries is excluded from backup. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

13. Sharing and links

We do not sell or share your personal data, because we do not receive any. Apart from Google's libraries described above, the app sends information only when you choose to:

  • Open a link, such as this privacy policy or another of our games on Google Play. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Contact us: the app opens your email app with our address filled in. You decide what to send.

14. Security

Because we do not collect your data, there is no copy of it on our side to protect or lose. Game data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Google states that the data its ad SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741 (1981), and U.S. state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal data. These can include the right to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • We do not hold personal data about you, so in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete. Your game data is on your device and you can delete it as described in section 11.
  • For data collected by Google, use your ad choices in the app (section 6), your device's ad settings (section 7), and Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can always contact us, and we will help as far as we are able.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app or the services it uses change. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also take reasonable steps to let you know, for example through the app's release notes.
